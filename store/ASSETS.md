# Asset provenance

- `extension/icons/mark.svg`: original geometric widescreen symbol, created for this project. No YouTube logo or third-party image.
- `extension/icons/icon*.png` and `store/icon128.png`: exports of the same screen geometry, generated by `scripts/generate-assets.py` with transparent backgrounds. The 128px icon has a 96px square mark and 16px padding.
- Promotional PNGs: original geometric artwork derived from that mark; no stock assets or generated illustrations.
- Screenshot PNGs: the actual popup HTML/CSS rendered in the local browser with a development-only storage adapter, placed on a designed 1280 × 800 explanatory canvas. The adapter does not ship in the extension. Captures demonstrate enabled/paused preference states, not a live YouTube player or store approval.

System typography is used. No remote fonts, tracking pixels or third-party asset licenses are required by the shipped extension.
